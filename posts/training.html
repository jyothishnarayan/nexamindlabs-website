// FAQ

Common questions

What are the batch sizes?

We keep batches small intentionally — max 8 for Pro courses and max 5 for Bright Future. This ensures every student gets proper attention and can ask questions freely.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing varies by course and duration. After you submit the enrolment form, we'll share a detailed breakdown within 24 hours based on your specific requirements.

Are sessions recorded?

Yes — all online Pro sessions are recorded and shared with enrolled students so you can revisit any topic at your own pace.

Can Bright Future students switch subjects?

Yes. At the start of each month you can adjust which subjects are being covered based on where your child needs the most support.

Is there a trial class?

We offer a free 30-minute demo session for both tracks so you can see the teaching style and ask questions before committing.

What if I'm not satisfied?

If you're not happy after the first two sessions, we'll refund your payment — no questions asked. We're confident in the quality but want you to have zero risk.
